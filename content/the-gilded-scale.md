A religious body in [[sanctuary-city|Sanctuary City]] that oversees the religious side of the city's economy. Its prelate, [[orsolya-venn|Orsolya Venn]], sits on the [[economic-council|Economic Council]] and dresses in yellow robes marked with religious inscriptions and emblems of office.

Nothing more is known yet. The night before the party met the council, hooded figures attacked churches and holy sites across the city.
