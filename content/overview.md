The heroes set out from [[broadhurst|Broadhurst]] for [[sanctuary-city|Sanctuary City]]. Getting in did not go cleanly: the party was split at the gates, and [[urzel-the-radiant|Urzel]] was only brought inside after the others murdered members of the [[ashgate-watch|Ashgate Watch]].

## Current Position

The party escaped the Watch, found [[annabel-swordbuck|Annabel Swordbuck]]'s smuggler's hide, and lost it again when the guard broke in. [[bertrude|Bertrude]] and [[torr|Torr]] stayed behind to hold the door. [[aurelion|Aurelion]], [[malthazar|Malthazar]] and [[urzel-the-radiant|Urzel]] escaped into the sewers and agreed to get Annabel's crew through them.

Beneath the city they found [[the-cistern|a cistern]] overrun by [[demons]], with bodies being sent over the edge into an abyss. A voice from the dark ordered the demons to "finish these fools". The heroes won, and among the bodies they found [[the-mawblade|the Mawblade]].

Sanctuary City is under assault by demons. Session 3 ended with the party still in the sewers, about to head back to civilisation.

## The Heroes

- [[aurelion]] — human censor, a former watch officer.
- [[bertrude]] — human telepath, a disavowed agent.
- [[malthazar]] — dragon knight shadow with a criminal past.
- [[torr]] — high elf tactician raised among the Wode Elves.
- [[urzel-the-radiant|Urzel the Radiant]] — orc conduit of Life and Sun.
