The seat of the [[economic-council|Economic Council]], in the centre of [[sanctuary-city|Sanctuary City]]. It's also called the Commerce Centre and serves as the city's town hall. It is a large, multi-storey bureaucratic mansion inside a walled, gated compound, and extremely wealthy-looking. The [[ashgate-watch|Ashgate Watch]] guard it heavily, and security has been raised because of the vagrants and miscreants loose in the city.

Inside, the massive doors open onto stairs, corridors and layer after layer of bureaucracy. A little servant in a vest plays a trumpet as visitors walk past. At the top is the inner sanctum where the council meets. It's a candlelit chamber with an ornate clock on the wall, full of attendants carrying scrolls and wine, with elite guards at the doors.

The party talked its way in during [[session-4|Session 4]].
