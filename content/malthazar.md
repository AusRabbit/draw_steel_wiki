## Inciting Incident — Atonement

The last criminal job Malthazar pulled led to the death of someone, or the destruction of something, they loved. To make up for the loss, they left their criminal ways behind and became a hero.

## Languages

Caelian, Vastariax, and I Speak Their Language (Criminal).

## Skills

Alchemy, Forgery, Mechanics, Gymnastics, Navigate, Lie, Intimidate, Read Person, Disguise, Eavesdrop, Sabotage, Sneak, Track, Criminal Underworld.

## Traits and Perks

- **Wings** — fly for a number of rounds equal to Might (minimum 1). While flying at levels 1–3, Malthazar has damage weakness 5.
- **Draconian Guard** — swing the wings round to guard against a blow.
- **Lucky Dog** — after failing an intrigue test, lose Stamina to improve the outcome by one tier.
- **Criminal Contacts** — during a respite in a settlement, ask the local criminal network a question.

## Class Features

- **Trained Assassin** — an extra surge on strikes that use surges.
- **Smoke Bomb** — hide even when observed, then shift away.
- **Coat the Blade** — "A little poison goes a long way."
- **Hesitation Is Weakness** — take a turn straight after another hero.
- **Defensive Roll** — roll with an enemy's attack to reduce the harm.
- **Sticky Bomb** — explosives are best when they're attached to an enemy.

## Abilities

- **Teamwork Has Its Place** (signature) — attack an enemy as an ally exposes its weakness.
- **Get In Get Out** (3 Insight) — move unexpectedly, strike fast and be gone.
- **One Hundred Throats** (5 Insight) — move across the battlefield and strike every foe within reach.
