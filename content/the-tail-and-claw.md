The party found the Tail and Claw during Session 1 in [[sanctuary-city|Sanctuary City]].

It has since become the party's base of operations, the place to regroup when they get separated. In [[session-4|Session 4]], [[torr|Torr]] and [[bertrude|Bertrude]] waited there until the others came back from the sewers, filthy and stinking.

The barkeep has opinions. He reckons the [[economic-council|Economic Council]] is a greedy bunch, but that if anyone can get an audience with them, it's this party.
