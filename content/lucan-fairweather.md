A member of the [[economic-council|Economic Council]] of [[sanctuary-city|Sanctuary City]]. He's podgy, with rings on his fingers and wine-stained lips, and attendants keep his cup filled. He tut-tutted when the party entered the council chamber and wouldn't look them in the eye the whole time.

## Session 4

When [[urzel-the-radiant|Urzel]] mentioned [[hugh|Hugh]], Lucan hiccupped at the name. [[idra-voss|Idra Voss]] noticed too. Later she asked him straight out to tell them who this Hugh is, and against his better judgement he started to answer. [[orsolya-venn|Orsolya Venn]] interrupted him before he could say much.
