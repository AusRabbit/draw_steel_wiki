## Inciting Incident — Disaster

A disaster hit Torr's crew while they were working. Torr saved as many as they could, but the ones they couldn't save weigh heavily on their mind. They took up the life of a hero to save as many people as possible, vowing that what happened would not happen again.

## Traits and Perks

- **High Senses** — an edge on tests to notice threats.
- **High Elf Glamor** — an edge on Presence tests using Flirt or Persuade; Torr appears slightly different to each observer, but always as themselves.
- **Unstoppable Mind** — immune to dazed.
- **Teamwork** — on the first turn of a montage test, make a test and assist another hero's.
- **Danger Sense** — in natural environments, an edge on Alertness, can't be surprised, and a warning of imminent natural disaster.

## Class Features

- **Studied Commander** — knowledge of battle makes war lore projects cheaper and can reveal what an encounter or negotiation holds.
- **Mark** — draw allies' attention to a specific foe.
- **"Strike Now!"** — point out an opening to an ally.
- **Overwatch** — an ally waits for the right moment to strike.
- **Goaded** — goad marked foes into acting before they're ready.

- **Patient Shot** (signature) — "Breathe ... aim ... wait ... then strike!"
