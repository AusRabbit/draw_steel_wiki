## Inciting Incident — Powerful Enemies

Aurelion made it their responsibility to root out and bring down the region's foremost crime syndicate. The syndicate sent goons to burn down their home and teach them a lesson, leaving them bleeding in the street with nothing left but their life. They have since taken on the life of a hero to gain the power and influence needed to destroy the syndicate once and for all.

## Traits and Perks

- **Detect the Supernatural** and **Resist the Unnatural** — human ancestry traits; **Staying Power** grants +2 Recoveries.
- **Team Leader** — spend a hero token at the start of a group or montage test so every participant tests as if they had the hero's exploration skills.
- **Engrossing Monologue** — outside combat, shout to hold the attention of non-hostile creatures within 10 squares; allies gain an edge to avoid being noticed while they listen.

## Class Features

- **Judgment** — a prayer that outlines a foe in holy energy and punishes it for acting.
- **My Life for Yours** — spend a Recovery to let yourself or an ally regain Stamina.
- **Lead by Example** and **Stalwart Example** — the gods' favour helps allies flank and aids tests to intimidate or persuade.
