Disgusting little [[demons]] with multiple tongues and bodies split horrifically open. Their stench is enough to make you gag, and saliva dribbles from tongue to cheek. They fight in packs led by a [[ruinant]], spitting acid from range, and fall quickly when hit, but they stack up if ignored.

Met in [[the-cistern|the Cistern]] in [[session-3|Session 3]]. Some scampered off into the tunnels.
