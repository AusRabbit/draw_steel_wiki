Humanoid in shape but made entirely of dripping, oily blood, solid only to a degree. Fiktins can transform into mundane objects: one waited underwater as a rock, and another burst out of the rags of a corpse. They were the strongest [[demons]] the party had faced.

They rose from [[the-cistern|the Cistern]] when a voice ordered the demons to "finish these fools", and they fought to the death. Met in [[session-3|Session 3]].
