## Holding the Door

Back at [[annabel-swordbuck|Annabel]]'s hide, [[torr|Torr]] and [[bertrude|Bertrude]] never had to fight. When the guard broke the door in, Torr complained that they were all very tired and asked whether the guard couldn't see that people were trying to sleep. The guard tore the place apart and found nothing. Bertie's mind tricks made the whole act convincing, and after a lot of questions Torr told them to kindly leave. They did. After the murders at the gate, the party could use a little restraint.

The two of them made their way back to [[the-tail-and-claw|the Tail and Claw]], the party's base of operations, and waited. Going into the sewers as a pair would have been unwise.

## Back at the Tail and Claw

Long after Torr had pulled up a seat, the others came through the door: wet, haggard, filthy and stinking. [[urzel-the-radiant|Urzel]] was delighted. Down below was a creature worth the challenge, and his god would be proud of the blood they spilled.

Annabel and her crew came with them. They had got her people through the sewers as promised. She went straight to the bar, ordered from the top shelf, spilled a bag of coins on the counter and told the barkeep to keep them coming. Then she buried herself in a bottle of wine. She flicked something disgusting off Urzel's shoulder and admitted that she thought she'd known everything, but the last few hours had shaken her. What were those things? Urzel told her he'd seen demons before: creatures of the night, coming out.

Her crew were just as deep in their cups. Her offsider, the dwarf, has the stone-like skin that dwarves in this realm are known for. He shook his head slightly, but she talked anyway.

## Hugh's Patron

Annabel knew about the party's friend [[hugh|Hugh]]. He'd been making quite the scene for himself in [[sanctuary-city|Sanctuary City]], and she hadn't been lying when she said she could help them find his patron. Hugh has friends in high places, perhaps the highest in the city. After the last few hours, though, she wasn't sure her information was worth anything any more, and she needed to confer with her colleagues.

She took [[malthazar|Malthazar]], who knows his way around the criminal element, on a scouting mission. He nodded sagely and wrapped his wings around himself. Her parting advice was that the rest of the party needed an audience with the council. Once she'd gone, the barkeep whistled from behind the bar. The [[economic-council|Economic Council]], he said, is a greedy bunch that rules this town. But if anyone could get an audience with them, it was this lot.

## The City Burns

The party took a respite. They woke the next morning to news that churches and holy sites had been attacked in the night by hooded figures. People were talking about cult activity, and there were fires all over the city, with the smoke hanging thick. The [[ashgate-watch|Ashgate Watch]] ran about more determined than ever to force control on a city that had started, ever so slightly, to slip.

## Getting an Audience

The Economic Council is rumoured to be four people, possibly more, and secrecy surrounds it. It sits in [[the-commerce-hall|the Commerce Hall]] at the centre of the city, with the Watch wrapped tight around it. Getting in took some work:

- **The one honest man.** Torr tried to bribe a mid-level bureaucrat who collects taxes in the market. Against all odds, Torr had found the one straight official in the city. He clutched his pearls at the very idea, and the party beat a hasty retreat before he could call the guard.
- **Tailing him.** Urzel followed him. The bureaucrat knew he was being watched and was no fool, but his errands showed the party his chain of command. His report would carry weight up that chain.
- **The dice den.** [[aurelion|Aurelion]] found part of that chain of command a few flights of stairs below ground, in a dank den of pipe smoke and sickly sweet drugs, rolling dice in the corner. He offered a wager: if he lost, the party would do some dirty work for them; if he won, they'd help him reach the people at the top. The gamblers bowed their heads reverently to Urzel, took the bet and promptly lost. They gave up the council's schedule. Most of its engagements had been cancelled, but it was certainly still meeting in the Commerce Hall.
- **"Gruul the One-Handed."** At the compound gate, Urzel told the guards that there is a darkness in this city, and that his god had sent him to cleanse it. Not every Ashgate guardsman is pious, but this one was. He took Urzel seriously in such troubled times and escorted the three of them through the compound himself. Beyond the massive doors, a little servant in a vest played a trumpet as they walked past.
- **Big-time players.** With Torr's help, Aurelion talked his way through yet more layers of bureaucracy: up the stairs, down the corridors, to an inner sanctum with still more people barring the way. The party were heroes, he said. They knew what was happening in the sewers and they wanted to help. "We've heard of people like you coming through the gates lately." The door opened a crack and a face appeared. After some whispering, they were let in.

## The Council Chamber

Four people waited inside:

- [[idra-voss|High Chancellor Idra Voss]], looking up from an endless scroll that tumbled off the table in front of her.
- [[brannoch-hale|Brannoch Hale]], in resplendent polished armour, with a withering stare and the badge of the Ashgate Watch on his chest.
- [[lucan-fairweather|Lucan Fairweather]], podgy, with rings on his fingers and wine-stained lips. He tut-tutted when they came in and wouldn't look at them.
- [[orsolya-venn|Orsolya Venn]], robed in yellow with religious inscriptions and emblems of office: the Prelate of [[the-gilded-scale|the Gilded Scale]].

Attendants scuttled about with scrolls and wine, and elite guards stood at the doors.

## The Negotiation

Urzel opened by saying that their dear friend Hugh had sent for them to help the city. Idra Voss had no idea who that was. But Urzel was watching the room, and he saw Lucan Fairweather hiccup at the name.

Torr then raised the creatures in the sewers. At that, Idra clapped twice and the attendants left the room, along with even some of the guards. She was behind schedule and fidgeting with her jewellery, eyeing the ornate clock on the wall. But she was interested. She also knew exactly who they were: outsiders who came through the Ashgate a few nights ago.

Then Aurelion reached behind his back and drew [[the-mawblade|the Mawblade]]. "Does this interest you?" It did. Idra's eyes sparkled, and so did her attendants'. Brannoch Hale was on his feet, reaching for the hilt of his own weapon. How dare they brandish a weapon in front of the council, and *that* thing of all items. Where had they found it? What was its nature?

Urzel stepped in front of the Lord Warden. Brannoch barred people of great religion and great light from entering, Urzel said, so either he knew more about what was going on in this city than he was letting on, or he was a fool.

Idra laid a hand on Brannoch's shoulder to cool things down. Her patience was nearly gone, so the party called in a favour. [[annabel-swordbuck|Annabel]] and Malthazar's early scouting had turned up something useful: a statue of Idra is going up in [[grain-square|Grain Square]], and it's enormous. She is vain, and her legacy matters to her. Urzel made his last pitch. Help the heroes defeat this great evil, and she'd be remembered as the saviour of the city and one of the greatest rulers who ever lived. Torr added that forgiveness can be found in gold.

## "I Signed a Deal with a Demon"

The theatrics were over. Idra sat down, made Brannoch sit as well, and took a few deep breaths. "It's true," she said. Brannoch and Lucan turned to look at her. Orsolya did not.

"The city has an infestation, and I know what it is." Brannoch shook his head and told her to trust the Ashgate Watch, that they could deal with it, and that she didn't have to sell herself out in front of these people. She ignored him. "I signed a deal with a demon many moons ago."

At the word *demon*, the candlelit room went dark and a breath of wind pushed through it.

She can never confess it in public; that is part of the bargain. Seasons ago, what seemed like a trifling deal saved the city from ruin and starvation. Now the demon, [[harkonics|Harkonics]], has proved insatiable, and it's getting out of hand. She admitted they should never have hunted the heroes through the streets like dogs. She asked their forgiveness and their help to save the city before it dies.

## The Shadow in the Room

Idra is perceptive too. She turned to Lucan, who still hadn't met anyone's eye, and asked him to tell them who this Hugh is. Against his better judgement, Lucan started to talk.

Orsolya Venn cut him off. The wind came back tenfold and blew the scrolls off the table. Orsolya wavered in front of them, and her shadow came away from her body and fragmented across the room as she began to chant a spell.

The session ended there, on the edge of a fight.
