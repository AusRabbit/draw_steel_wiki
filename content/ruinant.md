Not big, but a captain: each pack of lesser [[demons]] has a ruinant directing it, and the pack fights harder while it lives. Ruinants sprint through water as if it isn't there and attack with bloodletting claws.

In [[the-cistern|the Cistern]], a [[frenzied]] cracked and split from within to become a new ruinant, twice. One ruinant fled into the tunnels at the end of the fight. Met in [[session-3|Session 3]].
