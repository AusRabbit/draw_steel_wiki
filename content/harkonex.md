The demon that [[idra-voss|High Chancellor Idra Voss]] made a deal with many moons ago. In her words, Harkonex is "just insatiable".

The bargain was meant to save [[sanctuary-city|Sanctuary City]] from ruin and starvation. It looked trifling seasons ago, but it's now getting out of hand: it is the infestation of [[demons]] the party found beneath the city. Part of the bargain is that Idra can never confess it in public.

When she said the word *demon* in the council chamber, the candlelit room went dark and a breath of wind pushed through it.
