## Where Malthazar Went

[[malthazar|Malthazar]] had vanished during the chase through the back alleys. As he tells it, he caught sight of three thieves robbing a lone girl who looked like a rich man's daughter. He decided that couldn't happen on his watch, beat up all three, and then stole her money himself. By then he had lost the others.

He turned up at the door of [[annabel-swordbuck|Annabel]]'s hide shoving coins through the peephole, and her crew let him in. Money buys everything in a place like that.

The [[ashgate-watch|Ashgate Watch]] had been talking too. Guards were claiming they'd seen individuals with wings, and it had frightened them.

## The Hide Is Raided

Annabel introduced the party to her crew: three or four people, all nicknames and sideways glances, with a squat, gruff dwarf at her side who cared more about her safety than about anything in the street.

Then came a pounding at the only door: "Open up, it's the guard!" When the party looked round, Annabel and her crew had vanished. [[torr|Torr]] tried to talk the guard away through the door ("we're all very tired in here") and got nowhere. [[aurelion|Aurelion]] searched for where Annabel had gone and found nothing. Something heavier than a boot started breaking the door in.

Malthazar, who knows a thing or two about how smugglers build these places, found a false door hidden behind crates and worked out the mechanism just in time. The party slipped out, leaving behind a hide full of jewels, gems, weapons, drugs and booze. As the door fell in, [[bertrude|Bertrude]] and Torr shouted for the others to go on ahead while they held the door.

## Annabel's Bargain

The tunnel led into the [[sanctuary-city|Sanctuary City]] sewers. [[urzel-the-radiant|Urzel]], whose god is a god of fire and light, conjured a floating ball of light to see by.

They soon caught up with Annabel and her gang. She was not pleased. It was because of the party that the guard had found her hide, and months of smuggling work were gone. Then, with a glance behind her into the dark, she told them that things are amiss in Sanctuary City. The hide was so full because her people no longer trust the sewers: every group they send down doesn't come back. Her price was to have the party get her crew through the sewers, and maybe then they could talk.

## The Cistern

Hours of winding tunnels followed, with strange noises growing more frequent, until the party heard rushing water ahead. Annabel's crew hung back. Malthazar scouted ahead by sliding into the putrid sewage.

What he found was [[the-cistern|a cistern]] full of [[demons]]. None of the heroes had ever faced a demon before:

- [[pitling|Pitlings]] — stinking little things with many tongues and bodies split open, spitting acid.
- [[ruinant|Ruinants]] — the captains, each leading a pack.
- [[frenzied|Frenzied]] — fast, with huge claws and many teeth.
- [[torlas|Torlases]] — they turned the floor to spongy flesh and dragged Aurelion back and forth through it like a game of ping pong.
- A [[bendrak]] — like a horse with a strange head and gems floating around its body, which warped the heroes' perceptions and turned their blows against each other.

The fight was long and brutal. The demons proved weak to holy power. Twice a frenzied split and cracked open from within to become a new ruinant. Malthazar hid up in the piping and planted a sticky bomb on the bendrak, which eventually died in one last attempt to bend Aurelion's mind.

## "Finish These Fools"

A rumbling came from the centre of the cistern, then a voice, clear as day: "Finish these fools." Shapes rose from the water and hid among the pillars: [[fiktin|fiktins]], humanoid figures made of dripping, oily blood, the strongest creatures the party had faced. The remaining pitlings and a ruinant scampered off into the dark tunnels.

The party split up to explore while the fiktins hunted them:

- **The edge.** From the catwalk, Aurelion saw the water pour into a sinkhole that looked less like a sewer than a pit into another world. A woman's body, wrapped in rags, drifted over the edge and dwindled to a pinprick.
- **The bodies.** On a ledge lay an enormous mound of corpses, apparently being prepared to go over the edge. A fiktin burst from the rags of one of them.
- **The controls.** Urzel found sluice controls and drained the cistern. A rock that had been sliding around underwater turned out to be another fiktin.

The fiktins fought to the death, as though their lives had been ordered forfeit, and fell.

## The Mawblade

Among the bodies, wrapped in rags and kept apart from everything else bound for the edge, the party found a blade of black iron whose edge is lined with ivory teeth: [[the-mawblade|the Mawblade]]. There is more to learn about it.

## What It Means

Sanctuary City is under assault by demons. That is why no one goes into the sewers, why strange bodies are being pulled out of the canals, and why everything in the city has gone astray. At the table the heroes suspected that someone is controlling the demons.
