**Keywords:** Heavy Weapon, Magic

Any damage-dealing weapon ability using this weapon gains a +1 rolled damage bonus, and if the target is a giant, they are A<2 prone on a tier 3 result. When you are grabbed by a giant, you automatically escape at the start of your turn (no action).
