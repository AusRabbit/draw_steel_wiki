The High Chancellor of [[sanctuary-city|Sanctuary City]] and head of the [[economic-council|Economic Council]]. The party met her in the council chamber of [[the-commerce-hall|the Commerce Hall]] in [[session-4|Session 4]], where she sat behind an endless scroll that tumbled off the table in front of her.

She is busy, impatient and perceptive. When her patience runs low she fidgets with her jewellery and watches the ornate clock on the wall. She knew who the party were before they spoke: outsiders who came through the Ashgate a few nights ago.

## What Moves Her

The party found three things she cares about:

- **Protecting the city.** The mention of creatures in the sewers was enough to make her clear the room.
- **Power.** Her eyes sparkled when Aurelion drew [[the-mawblade|the Mawblade]].
- **Her legacy.** She's vain. A statue of her, a very large one, is going up in [[grain-square|Grain Square]].

## The Bargain

Under pressure, she confessed to the party what she can never confess in public: many moons ago she signed a deal with a demon. Seasons ago, it seemed a trifling price to save the city from ruin and starvation. Now the demon, [[harkonics|Harkonics]], has proved insatiable and the infestation is getting out of hand. Keeping it secret is part of the bargain.

She told the heroes they should never have been hunted through the streets like dogs, asked their forgiveness, and asked for their help to save the city.

She also seems to suspect [[lucan-fairweather|Lucan Fairweather]] knows more about [[hugh|Hugh]] than he's saying, and asked him outright.
