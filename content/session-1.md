## Leaving Broadhurst

The party set out from [[broadhurst|Broadhurst]], bound for [[sanctuary-city|Sanctuary City]].

## Refugees on the Road

On the road they fell in with refugees heading the same way, hopeful of what they would find in Sanctuary City.

## The Gates

At the city gates the party was interrogated and split up.

## Something Is Off

Inside, something about [[sanctuary-city|Sanctuary City]] did not sit right.

## The Side Gate

The party played out a scene at a side gate into the city.

## Getting Urzel In

The party found [[the-tail-and-claw|the Tail and Claw]] and interacted with the city's criminal elements. They stole some uniforms, and they murdered members of the [[ashgate-watch|Ashgate Watch]] in order to sneak [[urzel-the-radiant|Urzel]] into the city.
