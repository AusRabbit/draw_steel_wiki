A large cistern at a junction of the sewers under [[sanctuary-city|Sanctuary City]], crossed by boardwalks and a catwalk and full of knee-deep and deeper water. The party fought a horde of [[demons]] here in [[session-3|Session 3]].

## What the Party Found

- **The edge.** At the centre, the water pours into a sinkhole that looks like a pit into another world. Bodies drift over it and vanish.
- **The bodies.** A ledge holds an enormous mound of corpses, apparently being prepared to go over the edge. [[the-mawblade|The Mawblade]] was found among them.
- **The controls.** Sluice controls can close the grates and drain the cistern. [[urzel-the-radiant|Urzel]] used them.

A voice from the centre of the cistern ordered the demons to "finish these fools".
