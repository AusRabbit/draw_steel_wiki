[[sanctuary-city|Sanctuary City]] is ruled by an economic council. Rumour put it at four members, possibly more, and secrecy surrounds it. The barkeep at [[the-tail-and-claw|the Tail and Claw]] calls them a greedy bunch. They hold council in [[the-commerce-hall|the Commerce Hall]] at the centre of the city, with the [[ashgate-watch|Ashgate Watch]] wrapped tight around it. With the city in chaos, most of their engagements and events have been cancelled.

Reaching them means getting through layer after layer of bureaucracy. In [[session-4|Session 4]] that took a failed bribe, a tail, a game of dice and a pious guardsman.

## Members

- [[idra-voss|High Chancellor Idra Voss]] — head of the council.
- [[brannoch-hale|Brannoch Hale]] — Lord Warden of the Ashgate, in charge of the Watch.
- [[lucan-fairweather|Lucan Fairweather]] — knows something about [[hugh|Hugh]].
- [[orsolya-venn|Orsolya Venn]] — Prelate of [[the-gilded-scale|the Gilded Scale]].

## The Secret

Idra Voss told the party that she signed a deal with a demon, [[harkonex|Harkonex]], to save the city from ruin and starvation. That deal is the source of the [[demons]] in the sewers. Brannoch tried to stop her saying so.
