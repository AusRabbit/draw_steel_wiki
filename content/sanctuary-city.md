The city the party travelled to from [[broadhurst|Broadhurst]]. Refugees on the road were heading there in hope. The party was interrogated at its gates, and once inside found that something did not seem right.

The [[ashgate-watch|Ashgate Watch]] operates there. [[the-tail-and-claw|The Tail and Claw]] is in the city.

## Beneath the City

The sewers are something the city's smugglers no longer trust, because groups sent down don't come back. In [[session-3|Session 3]] the party found out why: [[the-cistern|a cistern]] full of [[demons]]. Sanctuary City is under assault by demons, which explains the empty sewers, the strange bodies being pulled out of the canals, and why everything in the city has gone astray.

## Who Rules It

Sanctuary City is ruled by the [[economic-council|Economic Council]] from [[the-commerce-hall|the Commerce Hall]] at its centre. The council's head is [[idra-voss|High Chancellor Idra Voss]], and [[brannoch-hale|Brannoch Hale]] commands the [[ashgate-watch|Ashgate Watch]]. The faith tied to the city's economy is [[the-gilded-scale|the Gilded Scale]]. A huge statue of Idra Voss is going up in [[grain-square|Grain Square]].

## The Truth of It

In [[session-4|Session 4]], Idra Voss confessed to the party in private that she signed a deal with a demon, [[harkonex|Harkonex]], seasons ago to save the city from ruin and starvation. The demon has proved insatiable. She can't admit it in public.

## The City on Fire

On the night after the party came up from the sewers, hooded figures attacked churches and holy sites across the city. There was talk of cult activity, fires everywhere and thick smoke, and a Watch struggling to keep its grip.
