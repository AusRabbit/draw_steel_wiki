## Alarm Bells

The killings did not go unnoticed. Alarm bells rang, the rest of the [[ashgate-watch|Ashgate Watch]] was alerted, and the party fled into the back alleys of [[sanctuary-city|Sanctuary City]].

## Through the Back Alleys

A chase through the alleys followed, and the party escaped the Watch.

## The Bolt Hole

The party found [[annabel-swordbuck|Annabel Swordbuck]] in a smuggler's bolt hole.
