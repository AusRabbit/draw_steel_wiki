A friend of the heroes. He's the reason they wanted [[annabel-swordbuck|Annabel Swordbuck]]'s help, and the name they used to talk their way in front of the [[economic-council|Economic Council]].

## What the Party Knows

- He made quite the scene for himself in [[sanctuary-city|Sanctuary City]].
- He has friends in high places, perhaps the highest in the city. Annabel believes she can help find out who his patron is, and she thinks the Economic Council will know where he can be found.
- [[idra-voss|High Chancellor Idra Voss]] had no idea who he was.
- [[lucan-fairweather|Lucan Fairweather]] hiccupped at his name and wouldn't meet the party's eyes. When Idra asked Lucan to explain who Hugh is, he started to answer, and [[orsolya-venn|Orsolya Venn]] interrupted. See [[session-4|Session 4]].
