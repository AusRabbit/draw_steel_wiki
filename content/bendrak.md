A [[demons|demon]] that looks like a horse with a strange head, with gems floating around its body and a long, skinny neck. It hangs back behind cover and warps its victims' perceptions. Someone under its influence finds their blows turned partly onto their friends. It died in [[the-cistern|the Cistern]] making one last attempt to bend [[aurelion|Aurelion]]'s mind. Met in [[session-3|Session 3]].
