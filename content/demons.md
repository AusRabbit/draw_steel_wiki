The party's first encounter with demons was in [[the-cistern|the Cistern]] in [[session-3|Session 3]]. None of the heroes had faced one before.

Sanctuary City is under assault by demons. That is why no one goes into the sewers, why strange bodies are being pulled out of the canals, and why everything in the city has gone astray.

## What the Party Has Learned

- They are weak to holy damage.
- A demon that is badly hurt fights more desperately, and becomes easier to hit.
- They answered to a voice from the centre of the cistern, which ordered them to "finish these fools".
