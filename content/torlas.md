[[demons|Demons]] that turn an area of ground into spongy flesh, which tears at anyone forced to move through it. They pull targets towards them with grasping tendrils. In [[the-cistern|the Cistern]], two of them dragged [[aurelion|Aurelion]] back and forth through the flesh and seemed to enjoy it. Met in [[session-3|Session 3]].
