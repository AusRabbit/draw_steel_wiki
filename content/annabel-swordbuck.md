An aloof elf, not a Wode Elf, with a nasty habit of picking her teeth with a thin shard of bone. The party found her in her smuggler's hide in [[sanctuary-city|Sanctuary City]] after escaping the [[ashgate-watch|Ashgate Watch]].

## Her Crew

Three or four people who go by nicknames. Her right hand is a squat, gruff dwarf with the stone-like skin that dwarves in this realm have. He cares more about her safety than about anything going on in the street.

## Session 3

When the guard raided the hide, Annabel and her crew slipped out through a false door and left the party behind. The hide held months of smuggling work, including jewels, gems, weapons, drugs and booze, and the raid cost her all of it. She blames the party.

She told them that things are amiss in [[sanctuary-city|Sanctuary City]]: her people no longer trust the sewers, because every group they send down doesn't come back. Her price was for the party to get her crew through the sewers, and maybe then they could talk. She and her crew hung back while the party went into [[the-cistern|the Cistern]].

## Session 4

The party got her crew through the sewers as promised. Back at [[the-tail-and-claw|the Tail and Claw]], she drank top-shelf with a bag of coins on the bar and admitted that what she'd seen below had shaken her.

She knows about the party's friend [[hugh|Hugh]]. He has friends in high places, perhaps the highest in the city, and she believes she can help find his patron. She went off scouting the city's criminal element with [[malthazar|Malthazar]] and told the others to get an audience with the [[economic-council|Economic Council]]. Despite being criminals, she and her people call this city home. Their early findings, including the statue of [[idra-voss|Idra Voss]] going up in [[grain-square|Grain Square]], helped the party win the High Chancellor over.
