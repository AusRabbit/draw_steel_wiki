The party murdered several members of the Ashgate Watch to sneak [[urzel-the-radiant|Urzel]] into [[sanctuary-city|Sanctuary City]]. Alarm bells followed, and the rest of the Watch was alerted. The party escaped them in the back alleys.

Members of the Watch have since been claiming they saw individuals with wings, and it frightened them. In [[session-3|Session 3]] the guard raided [[annabel-swordbuck|Annabel]]'s smuggler's hide while the party was inside.

## Session 4

The Watch is commanded by [[brannoch-hale|Brannoch Hale]], the Lord Warden of the Ashgate, who sits on the [[economic-council|Economic Council]]. The Ashgate itself is the gate the party came through into the city a few nights earlier. The Watch guards the council heavily in [[the-commerce-hall|the Commerce Hall]].

[[torr|Torr]] and [[bertrude|Bertrude]] talked the guards who raided [[annabel-swordbuck|Annabel]]'s hide into leaving empty-handed. The morning after the churches burned, the Watch was running about more determined than ever to force control on the city. Not every guardsman is pious, but one at the council's gate was, and he escorted [[urzel-the-radiant|Urzel]] inside himself.

In the council chamber, [[idra-voss|High Chancellor Idra Voss]] admitted the heroes should never have been hunted through the streets like dogs.
