### Orsolya's spell
`hot` Cliffhanger

As [[lucan-fairweather|Lucan Fairweather]] began to explain who [[hugh|Hugh]] is, [[orsolya-venn|Orsolya Venn]] interrupted. Wind tore through the council chamber, her shadow came away from her body and fragmented across the room, and she began to chant a spell. Session 4 ended on the edge of a fight.

### Idra Voss's bargain
`hot` Confessed

[[idra-voss|High Chancellor Idra Voss]] admitted in private that she signed a deal with a demon, [[harkonics|Harkonics]], to save [[sanctuary-city|Sanctuary City]] from ruin and starvation. The demon is insatiable and the infestation is getting out of hand. She can't confess it in public, and she has asked for the heroes' help.

### Where is Hugh?
`hot` Lucan knows something

The party's friend [[hugh|Hugh]] has a patron in the highest places in the city. Idra Voss didn't know the name, but Lucan Fairweather hiccupped when he heard it and was about to talk when he was interrupted.

### The burning churches
`hot` New

The night after the party came up from the sewers, hooded figures attacked churches and holy sites across the city. People are talking about cult activity.

### The bodies and the abyss
`open` Unexplained

In [[the-cistern|the Cistern]], an enormous mound of corpses was being prepared to go over the edge into a sinkhole that looks like a pit into another world. Strange bodies are also being pulled out of the city's canals.

### Who commands the demons?
`open` Partly answered

A voice from the centre of the Cistern ordered the [[demons]] to "finish these fools". Idra Voss's deal with Harkonics explains why the demons are here, but not whose voice that was.

### The Mawblade
`open` Recognised

[[aurelion|Aurelion]] carries [[the-mawblade|the Mawblade]]. When he drew it in the council chamber, Idra Voss's eyes sparkled and [[brannoch-hale|Brannoch Hale]] reached for his own weapon: "That thing of all items." He demanded to know where they found it and what its nature is.

### Annabel and Malthazar
`open` Scouting

[[annabel-swordbuck|Annabel]] and [[malthazar|Malthazar]] are working the city's criminal element for information on Hugh and the crisis. Their early findings helped win Idra Voss over.

### The Ashgate Watch
`open` Uneasy truce?

The party murdered members of the [[ashgate-watch|Ashgate Watch]] to get [[urzel-the-radiant|Urzel]] into the city. Now Idra Voss says the heroes should never have been hunted like dogs. The Watch's Lord Warden, Brannoch Hale, is not so sure.

### Demons that got away
`open` Loose

Some [[pitling|pitlings]] and a [[ruinant]] scampered off into the dark tunnels during the fight in the Cistern.

### Bertrude and Torr
`closed` Safe in Session 4

[[bertrude|Bertrude]] and [[torr|Torr]] talked the guard away from [[annabel-swordbuck|Annabel]]'s hide without a fight and waited for the others at [[the-tail-and-claw|the Tail and Claw]].

### Annabel's bargain
`closed` Paid in Session 4
