### Who commands the demons?
`hot` New

A voice from the centre of [[the-cistern|the Cistern]] ordered the [[demons]] to "finish these fools", and the [[fiktin|fiktins]] fought to the death as though ordered to. At the table the heroes suspected someone is controlling them.

### The bodies and the abyss
`hot` Unexplained

In the Cistern, an enormous mound of corpses was being prepared to go over the edge into a sinkhole that looks like a pit into another world. Strange bodies are also being pulled out of the city's canals.

### Bertrude and Torr
`hot` Separated

[[bertrude|Bertrude]] and [[torr|Torr]] stayed behind to hold the door of [[annabel-swordbuck|Annabel]]'s hide against the guard so the others could escape. Nobody knows what happened to them.

### The Mawblade
`open` More to learn

The party found [[the-mawblade|the Mawblade]] among the bodies, wrapped in rags and set apart. The Director has promised to tell them more about it.

### Annabel's bargain
`open` Unpaid

Annabel blames the party for the loss of her hide. Her price was for them to get her crew through the sewers, and maybe then they could talk. She and her crew were waiting back in the tunnels while the party fought in the Cistern.

### The Ashgate Watch
`hot` Hunting the party

The party murdered members of the [[ashgate-watch|Ashgate Watch]] to get [[urzel-the-radiant|Urzel]] into the city. Guards are now telling stories about winged individuals, and the guard has raided Annabel's hide.

### Demons that got away

Some [[pitling|pitlings]] and a [[ruinant]] scampered off into the dark tunnels during the fight in the Cistern.
