A square in [[sanctuary-city|Sanctuary City]]. A statue of [[idra-voss|High Chancellor Idra Voss]] is being built there, and its sheer size shows how much she cares about her legacy. [[annabel-swordbuck|Annabel]] and [[malthazar|Malthazar]] turned this up while scouting in [[session-4|Session 4]], and the party used it to win her over.
