## Inciting Incident — Dogma

Urzel joined a religious institution under the guidance of a kind mentor, but others in the house of worship grew increasingly fanatical. The mentor tried to be a voice of reason, and was tried as a heretic and executed. Urzel left the institution and became a hero to uphold the beliefs they hold dear.

## Traits and Perks

- **Relentless** — when knocked to dying, make a free strike; dropping a foe lets Urzel spend a Recovery.
- **Nonstop** — immune to slowed. **Grounded** — +1 Stability.
- **Ritualist** — a one-minute blessing gives a double edge on the target's next test.

## Class Features

- **Prayer** — pray for extra piety at the risk of angering the gods.
- **The Lists of Heaven** — healing an ally lets Urzel spend a Recovery too.
- **Revitalizing Ritual** and **Inner Light** — respite blessings that boost recoveries and saving throws.
- **Healing Grace** — divine energy restores the righteous.
- **Ray of Wrath** — a blast of holy light.
- **Word of Guidance** — invigorate an attacking ally with divine energy.

## Abilities

- **Blessed Light** (signature) — burning radiance that shares the target's energy with an ally.

- **Morning Light** (5 Piety) — burn foes and bless allies.
- **Faith Is Our Armor** (5 Piety) — the heroes' armour glows with golden light.
