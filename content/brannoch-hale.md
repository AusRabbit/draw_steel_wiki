A member of the [[economic-council|Economic Council]] of [[sanctuary-city|Sanctuary City]], in resplendent polished armour and with a withering stare. The badge on his chest marks him as the man responsible for the [[ashgate-watch|Ashgate Watch]], the Lord Warden of the Ashgate. Given how the party got into the city, he is not their biggest friend in the room.

## Session 4

When [[aurelion|Aurelion]] drew [[the-mawblade|the Mawblade]] in the council chamber, Brannoch was on his feet at once with a hand on the hilt of his own weapon. "How dare you brandish a weapon here in front of us. And that thing of all items." He wanted to know where they had found it and what its nature was. He seems to know something about it.

[[urzel-the-radiant|Urzel]] challenged him: either he knew more about what was going on in the city than he was letting on, or he was a fool.

When [[idra-voss|Idra Voss]] began to confess, Brannoch tried to stop her. The Ashgate Watch could deal with this, whatever it was, and she didn't have to sell herself out in front of these people. She made him sit down and told them anyway.
