## Inciting Incident — Disavowed

On a dangerous espionage assignment, things went sideways. Bertrude escaped with their life, but the mission was a public failure thanks to bad information their agency gave them. The agency denied they worked for it, and Bertrude went on the run. Hero work will let them survive and clear their name.

## Class Features

- **Telepathic Speech** — speak mind to mind with any creature within Mind Spike's range that shares a language.
- **Ease the Mind** — an edge on tests to stop combat and start a negotiation, and hostile or suspicious NPCs have more patience.
- **Mind Spike** — a telepathic bolt that instantly zaps a creature's brain.
- **Remote Assistance** — an ally gains the benefit of your intellect.
- **Feedback Loop** — a brief psychic link gives an enemy a taste of its own medicine.

- **Flashback** (5 Clarity) — throw a target several seconds back through time so it can act again.
