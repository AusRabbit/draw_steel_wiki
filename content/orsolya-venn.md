A member of the [[economic-council|Economic Council]] of [[sanctuary-city|Sanctuary City]], robed in yellow with religious inscriptions and emblems of office. She is the Prelate of [[the-gilded-scale|the Gilded Scale]], responsible for the religious side of the city's economy.

## Session 4

When [[idra-voss|Idra Voss]] said "it's true" and began her confession, everyone looked at her except Orsolya.

When [[lucan-fairweather|Lucan Fairweather]] began to tell the party about [[hugh|Hugh]], Orsolya interrupted. Wind tore through the room and blew the scrolls off the table. She wavered in front of the heroes, and her shadow came away from her body and fragmented across the room as she began to chant a spell. The session ended there.

The night before, churches and holy sites across the city had been attacked by hooded figures.
