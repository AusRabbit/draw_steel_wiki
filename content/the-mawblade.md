**Keywords:** Magic, Weapon

You sense power but you're not sure what this weapon will do when you use it in battle!

## History

Found in [[the-cistern|the Cistern]] in [[session-3|Session 3]]. It was wrapped in rags and kept apart from the other items and debris that were going over the edge with the bodies.

The Director has promised to tell the party more about it.

## Session 4

[[aurelion|Aurelion]] carries it. In the council chamber of [[the-commerce-hall|the Commerce Hall]], he drew it from behind his back and asked the council, "Does this interest you?"

It did. [[idra-voss|High Chancellor Idra Voss]]'s eyes sparkled at the sight of it, and so did her attendants'. [[brannoch-hale|Brannoch Hale]] was on his feet with a hand on his own weapon: "That thing of all items." He demanded to know where they had found it and what its nature was. He seems to recognise it.
